# Data Processing Addendum — Beta Period

**Effective:** May 12, 2026
**Last updated:** May 12, 2026

---

## 1. Scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") forms part of the agreement
between **Super Mega Lab LLC** ("**Processor**" or "**Super Mega Lab**")
and the customer identified in the applicable order or end user
license agreement ("**Customer**" or "**Controller**") (together, the
"**Agreement**") and applies to Processor's Processing of Personal
Data on behalf of Customer in connection with the Services.

**Effective Date:** The date Customer accepts the Agreement or the
date this DPA is countersigned, whichever is later.

## 2. Beta Period — no Processing

During the Beta Period of the Services, Processor does not Process
any Personal Data on behalf of Customer. The Software runs entirely
on the user's device and does not transmit Personal Data to
Processor's infrastructure. Specifically, Processor does not receive,
store, or have access to:

- Source code, file contents, file paths, or file names from
  Customer's development environment;
- Repository names, remote URLs, branch names, commit hashes, commit
  messages, or other version control metadata;
- Prompts, AI completions, model inputs or outputs, or assistant
  conversations;
- Editor activity, keystrokes, telemetry events, behavioral
  analytics, or product usage events;
- Diagnostics, error reports, or crash dumps;
- Repository visibility status;
- IP addresses or other connection metadata.

Because no Personal Data is Processed on behalf of Customer during
the Beta Period, the substantive obligations described in a full Data
Processing Addendum (Customer Instructions, Sub-processors, Data
Subject Rights, Security Incident Notification, International Data
Transfers, Return and Deletion) do not apply during the Beta Period.

## 3. End of the Beta Period

A full Data Processing Addendum will be published at https://agenticbookmarks.com/legal/dpa when
the subscription verification service launches. Customer will be
notified at least **thirty (30) days** in advance.

## 4. Confidentiality

To the extent Processor's personnel become aware of any information
about Customer or Customer's end users through support communications
or other limited interactions during the Beta Period, Processor shall
ensure that such personnel are bound by appropriate confidentiality
obligations.

## 5. Governing Law

Except where mandatory data protection law requires otherwise, this
DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA.

## 6. Notices

Notices under this DPA shall be sent to:

- **Processor:** Super Mega Lab LLC, Legal Department,
  contact@supermegalab.com, 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, Delaware
  19958
- **Customer:** as set out in the Agreement

## 7. Order of Precedence

In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this DPA and
the Agreement with respect to Processing of Personal Data, this DPA
controls.

## 8. Term

This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date and continues until the
Agreement terminates or expires, or until superseded by the full DPA
published at the end of the Beta Period.
